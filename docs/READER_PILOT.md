# Larkspin founding-reader pilot

## Cohort and invitation link

- Cohort: `founding-readers-2026-08`
- Link: `https://larkspin.com/?pilot=founding-readers-2026-08`
- Size: 8–12 readers who enjoy fiction but did not help build Larkspin
- Window: seven days

The `pilot` query value is stored only for the browser session. It is attached
to bounded product-event metadata and the optional feedback form, never to
story prose. The Publisher's Ledger reports this cohort separately.

## The reader's assignment

1. Open the invitation link on a phone or computer.
2. Choose any built-in world and create a protagonist.
3. Read until you naturally want to stop; do not force completion.
4. Use **Pilot feedback** at any point. Say where you stopped, rate the
   experience, answer the $3.99 question, and leave one candid note.

Do not coach readers through the interface. Confusion is a result, not a test
failure to hide.

## Decision thresholds

Treat the pilot as directional, not statistically conclusive.

- At least 8 distinct readers select a world.
- At least 70% of selectors complete character setup.
- At least 60% of starters continue beyond chapter one.
- At least 35% of starters finish a story.
- Average submitted rating is at least 4.0/5.
- At least half of respondents answer yes to $3.99 for another story.
- No repeated account, library, checkout, or reader-navigation failure.

If setup conversion misses, fix onboarding before acquisition. If continuation
misses, inspect chapter length, page navigation, and the first choice. If
completion misses but ratings are strong, test a shorter target length before
changing story architecture. Do not enable live charging solely because the
price-interest question passes.

## Daily operation

Open **Publisher's ledger → Reader pilots** and record the cohort's selectors,
starters, finishers, feedback count, average rating, and willingness-to-pay.
Read feedback for recurring themes, but do not optimize around a single note.
After seven days, archive the results and decide: proceed, revise and repeat,
or stop.
