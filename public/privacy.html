Who this applies to

Plotwick is intended for adults. You must be at least [MINIMUM AGE — e.g. 18, or 13 with the disclosures your jurisdiction requires] to create an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below that age; if you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Information we collect

  • Account information. Your email address and authentication credentials, handled through our authentication and database provider (Supabase). Passwords are stored by that provider using industry-standard hashing; we never see your plaintext password.
  • Content you create. The worlds, characters, prompts, choices, free-text actions, and resulting stories you generate and choose to save to your cloud library. Stories you keep only on your device are stored in that browser’s local storage and are not sent to us until you sign in and import them.
  • Payment information. When you buy Wicks, payment is processed by Stripe. We do not receive or store your full card number. We store purchase identifiers, amounts, currency, product purchased, payment status, and the resulting Wick grants — for fulfillment, reconciliation, refunds, and support.
  • Usage and product events. Limited events such as selecting a world, beginning or completing a story, and opening or completing checkout. These are keyed to your account when signed in, or to a salted session hash otherwise, and do not contain your story text.
  • Reader-pilot feedback. If you join an invited pilot, we store the cohort label, how far you read, your rating and price-interest answer, and the feedback note you choose to submit. It is keyed like other product events and does not automatically attach your story text.
  • Technical and log data. Information needed to operate and protect the Service, such as IP address, approximate request metadata, and rate-limit counters. Where we retain identifiers tied to abuse reports, we store a salted hash of the reporter’s IP address rather than the raw address.
  • Model-usage records. For each generation we record metadata (request, account, story, model, endpoint, status, and token counts) to protect the Service, investigate failures, and understand generation costs. These records do not include your story text.

How we use information

  • To provide the Service: sign you in, generate and stream your stories, save your library, and sync it across your devices.
  • To process payments, grant and reconcile Wicks, and provide support.
  • To secure the Service: enforce rate limits, detect and prevent abuse, fraud, and misuse, and investigate incidents.
  • To understand aggregate product usage and generation costs so we can operate and improve Plotwick.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

AI generation

Plotwick creates stories with a third-party AI provider (currently Anthropic, PBC). Please do not include sensitive personal information (for example, government IDs, health, financial, or precise-location details) in a prompt or action. Your use of AI-generated output is also governed by our Terms. Service providers and third parties

We share information only with providers that help us run the Service, under their terms, and only as needed:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and storage of accounts, libraries, ledger, and rate-limit data.
  • Stripe — payment processing. Stripe handles your card details directly under its own privacy policy.
  • Anthropic — AI generation of story text and optional cover art.
  • Hosting/infrastructure — the platform that runs the Service (currently Railway).

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use third-party advertising trackers. Sharing and published stories

A story is private to your account unless you choose to publish it. When you publish, anyone with the link can read that story and its cover until you unpublish it. Search engines or others may cache a public link while it is live. Published stories may be reported by readers and removed if they violate our Terms or create safety, legal, or operational risk.
